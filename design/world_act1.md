# World and story: act 1

Everything here was settled in design conversation on 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01. Later acts exist in outline only and are out of scope.

## Premise

A normal villager in Tjärnäs, a viking village on a lake in the middle of a Swedish forest. Not a warrior, not chosen. More curious than the others, and willing to go where they won't.

## Tjärnäs

- An inland lake village with forest on every side. Fishing, hunting, small farming. The lake is just called Sjön; the mountain to the north is Berget.
- The fog is treated like weather. When fog shows on the lake, a horn sounds from the shore and everyone comes home. Boats are pulled up, doors barred. People are worried the fog is coming more often, but they get by day to day and think it's beyond anyone's control.
- The rumor: the fog is dangerous because of the vittror. Anything uncanny in the forest is called oknytt, one word for all of it, which is the whole misunderstanding.
- Nobody investigates, except the player and the old lady.

## The old lady

Lives at the edge of Tjärnäs where the last fence meets the trees. Healer and wise woman. Keeps old customs nobody remembers the reason for, like warning "those below" before pouring hot water on the ground. The village dismisses her: she's getting too old, she doesn't know what she's talking about. She is the only one who believes the player. Too old and stiff to travel, she stays behind. Before the player goes north she gives them her lantern. Name is still open (suggestion: Ragnfrid).

## The vittror

Underground folk from Swedish folklore. They live underground, in the forest, and around humans, who normally can't see or speak with them. In this game they are in the forest far more than usual because their home in the snowy mountains far to the north is threatened by a force nobody understands. Their presence is the fog. They keep herds, spirit-elk with antlers, whose bells are heard in the fog with no cattle in sight. They are victims, not villains.

## The frost and the possessed

The vittror unintentionally brought south the bad spirits and energy that drove them from their home. It shows as frost that doesn't melt, and it possesses animals of the forest and people who, over the years, got lost in the forest or the fog and died there. Possessed creatures attack on sight. Everyone, including the player at first, assumes the vittror are doing it on purpose.

Rules of thumb:

- Fog is theirs; frost is its.
- Possessed humans are background. Nobody in the village needs to know they exist, and there is no cure plot.
- The force itself is never named or explained in act 1.

## Reveals

1. **End of phase 1.** Fog falls on the way home from a routine hunt. Bells, then voices. The vittror speak to a human for the first time in ages. They beg for help and say they stay here because their home is threatened.
2. **End of phase 2.** The vittror say the cold followed them south, they never meant to bring it, and it takes whatever dies in it. That is what the possessed are. The only way to end it is at their home. Their road home runs under Berget, and something big has taken the cave.

## Ending of act 1

The village still blames the vittror. Only the player and the old lady think otherwise, and she is dismissed as senile. The player leaves lonely but with her warmth. She stays in Tjärnäs. The farewell is at the last fence, at night, with the lantern. No feast, and the headman doesn't come. The player goes north alone through the troll's cave. Act 2 is tall mountains, constant snowstorms and mountain caves, and is out of scope.

## Three phases

1. **Ordinary days.** Routine tasks, the first possessed animals by daylight, the old lady's ritual, then the fog and the first conversation.
2. **Questions.** Investigating zones for why the vittror are here: the runes in Fairmeadow, the herd in the fog, the cairn path, Norrby, the frost line. Ends with the second reveal.
3. **Preparation.** Gear for the north (winter coat, lantern), clearing the approach, the troll at Berget's mouth.

## Signposts

Day signposts read Sjön and Berget. Fog signposts read Norr, Skogen, Ingen väg and Frost. In fog the day signs are hidden and the fog signs shown, in different places.

## Glossary

- **Tjärnäs**: the village. **Sjön**: the lake. **Berget**: the mountain to the north with the cave.
- **Norrby**: the smaller village at the north end of the lake.
- **Fairmeadow**: the meadow with goldieflowers and runestones.
- **Goldieflowers**: the flower the old lady needs for her ritual.
- **Oknytt**: the village's word for anything uncanny in the forest.
- **Vittror** (one vittra): the underground folk. **Spirit-elk**: their herd animals.
- **Cairn**: a stone waypoint with a lantern. Lit cairns are safe markers in fog.
- **The horn**: sounded from the shore when fog is seen. **The bell**: phase 2 reward, rings toward the nearest cairn. **The lantern**: the old lady's, phase 3 reward. **The winter coat**: phase 3 reward, frost resistance.
